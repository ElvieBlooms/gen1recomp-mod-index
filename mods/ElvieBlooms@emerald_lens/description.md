Play the Gen 3 games as someone new.

Emerald Lens swaps the player's sprites in FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, everywhere the game draws them: walking, running, biking, surfing and fishing, your battle back pic and throw, the front pic, the trainer card, the new-game intro and the map icon. On FireRed and LeafGreen it covers riding the bird in Fly, too. It's the Gen 3 companion to [Crystal Lens](https://github.com/dburton95/crystal) (listed here as Crystal Player Mod), built on its framework with Dgray66's permission, and it uses the same character folders.
